Magium Text — Privacy Policy

About this policy

This privacy policy applies to Magium - text adventure RPG, also called Magium Text or Magium Text Edition, Android package org.magium.textedition. The app is provided by Flori Dumitrescu under the Google Play developer name Jessica Wilson Magium. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Data collection and sharing

The Android app works offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data with the developer or third parties. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. No account or sign-in is required. The current Android app does not request Internet permission.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, choices, character statistics, achievements, save names, and reading preferences are stored locally on your device so that you can continue playing. This information is not uploaded to a developer server. The app does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

Importing and exporting saves

If you choose to import a backup, the app reads the file you select through the system file picker. If you choose to export a backup, it writes your game data to the destination you select. These actions are initiated by you; the app does not automatically send backup files to the developer. A cloud storage provider you select may handle the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the app, so keep them in a location you trust.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it. To remove the app’s local data, use Android Settings, open this app’s storage settings, and choose Clear data or Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing data or uninstalling removes local progress, so export any saves you want to keep first. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. There are no app accounts or developer-held gameplay records to delete.

External services and links

The menu and credits may open external websites, including Reddit, GitHub, and DeviantArt, in your browser. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them. Google Play and Android may process information independently when you install, update, or use your device under Google’s own policies. Visiting the public policy webpage also involves its hosting provider. These services are separate from the app’s offline local storage.

Contacting the developer

For privacy questions, contact Flori Dumitrescu at floridum@gmail.com. If you choose to send an email or a bug report, the email address and information you provide are used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Such correspondence is retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of that correspondence. Do not include passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. Any future features that change data handling will be reflected in this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration.
