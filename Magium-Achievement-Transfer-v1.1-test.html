Achievement Transfer

Bring your earned achievements from Cristian’s original Magium to Magium Text Edition.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. No data is uploaded. Nothing in either game or your Google account is changed.

1. Original game data

Go to Google Takeout, deselect all products, select Google Play Games Services, then create a one-time ZIP export using the Google account you used for Magium. Download the ZIP when it is ready.

Only the original Magium achievement file inside the archive is read. If there is no cloud backup, this tool cannot recover achievements.

2. Current Magium Text backup

In Magium Text, export a fresh backup using the game's backup menu. Keep that original JSON file safe. Do not play further until you have imported the merged backup.

Your current saved games, checkpoints and achievements are preserved in the new copy.

3. Review and download

Important: downloading is not the final step.
  1. Tap Download merged backup below. If your browser offers to open the downloaded file, do not tap "Open file".
  2. Open Magium Text → Menu → Import backup.
  3. Select Magium-Text-Merged-Backup.json from your Downloads folder.
  4. Check your achievements and saved games.

Your achievements will not change until you import the merged file. Keep your original backup.
